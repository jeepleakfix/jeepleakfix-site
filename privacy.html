Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

JeepLeakFix.com ("we," "I," Tony) respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, what information the site collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. The short version: I only use your information to help solve your Jeep's water leak, and I never sell it.

Information you give me

When you submit the diagnosis form, you choose to share details about your Jeep and how to reach you:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your Jeep's year, model, number of doors, and roof type
  • Where and when it leaks, plus any details you add

I use this only to reply to you personally with a recommended fix, and to follow up about your specific leak.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, this site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use the pages — for example, which pages are viewed and general device, browser, and approximate location data. This is collected through cookies and is used in aggregate to improve the site. The site also uses Cloudflare Turnstile to block spam submissions on the form.

How your information is used

  • To respond to you personally with a fix for your Jeep
  • To improve the content and helpfulness of the site
  • To keep the site secure and free of spam

Who it's shared with

I do not sell or rent your information. It is handled by the trusted services that run the site: Google (Analytics), GitHub (hosting), Cloudflare (spam protection), and Google Workspace/Gmail (email). If you purchase a kit, that happens on our separate store at leakfixkit.com, which has its own checkout and privacy terms.

Your choices

  • You can ask me to access or delete the information you submitted at any time — just email jeepleakfix@gmail.com.
  • You can opt out of analytics cookies through your browser settings or a tracking-blocker.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email jeepleakfix@gmail.com or call/text +1-828-591-0302.

This policy is provided in good faith as a plain-language summary of how the site handles information. It is not legal advice. If you have specific legal obligations (for example under GDPR or CCPA), please have a qualified professional review it for your situation.
